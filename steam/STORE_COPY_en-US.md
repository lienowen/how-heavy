# The Weight We Carry

## One-line pitch

Trade weight between yourself and the world to leap farther, stand against impossible winds, and break open paths that were never meant to yield.

## Short description

The Weight We Carry is an atmospheric puzzle-platformer about exchanging weight. Become light enough to cross a chasm, heavy enough to resist the storm, and decide what you are willing to leave behind.

## About this game

Weight is not a stat. It is the rule that shapes every step.

In a silent measuring hall built to judge what people carry, you can trade your weight with marked stones and ancient mechanisms. A lighter body leaps higher and hangs in the air—but the wind can take it. A heavier body moves with purpose, withstands force, and can shatter fragile structures—but every jump becomes a commitment.

Read the room. Choose what to carry. Turn the same simple exchange into a new solution each time.

### Features

- Trade weight instantly between your character and objects in the environment.
- Shift between light, balanced, and heavy movement with clear physical consequences.
- Cross chasms, survive wind corridors, and break open hidden routes.
- Explore a quiet, hand-crafted world where mechanics and story speak the same language.
- Recover quickly from mistakes with forgiving checkpoints and instant retries.
- Play with keyboard or Xbox-style controller.
- Fully playable offline.
